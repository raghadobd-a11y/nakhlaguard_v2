📖 How to Use NakhlaGuard

A quick guide to every part of the system.

🎙️ 1. Run a live test

  1. Enter a Palm ID and upload a trunk audio recording (WAV).
  2. Press 'Analyze now'.
  3. See the severity result — for moderate/advanced cases, tap the sections below it for a full AI treatment report.

Tip: location and age fields are optional — 'Use current' and '📷 Estimate from photo' fill them automatically.

🌴 2. Palm Registry

Register every palm on your farm once (ID, location, age, notes). Every test you run automatically updates that palm's status here, so you can see your whole farm's health at a glance.

🔔 3. Farmer Alert Dashboard

Keep this page open (on your phone or computer) while working the farm. Tap 'Enable sound & browser notifications' once, and you'll get an instant alert the moment any palm's test comes back moderate or advanced — no need to refresh.

📞 Backup Contact

On the alert dashboard, set a name and phone number for someone who can act if you're unavailable (traveling, out of the country). This contact is shown automatically on every future risk alert, so urgent cases don't go unanswered.

🤖 4. AI Farm Assistant

Ask it anything in plain language: 'Which palms need attention?', 'How old is Palm-A12?', or general questions about Red Palm Weevil. It looks up your real farm data on its own before answering.

🎨 Understanding the risk colors

Every result across the app uses the same color code:

Green = Healthy
Amber = Early stage
Orange = Moderate
Red = Advanced
